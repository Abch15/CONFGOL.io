Author Guidelines

Paper Preparation

Submitted papers must present original unpublished research that is not currently under review for any other conference or journal. Full papers must be in English and submitted in PDF format (range of 6-10 pages).

Authors are kindly advised to follow the Springer Proceedings templates for their manuscripts.

Copyright & Permissions

Authors of accepted papers must sign the Springer copyright form (License to Publish). Authors wishing to include previously published figures or tables must obtain permission from the copyright owners.

Submission Process

To submit your paper, please access the online submission system. Ensure your paper is appropriately structured and received before the due date.
