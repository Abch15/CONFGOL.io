Keynote Speakers

Dmitry Ivanov

Berlin School of Economics and Law, Germany

Prof. Dr. Dr. habil. Dmitry Ivanov is Professor of Supply Chain and Operations Management, and director of the Digital-AI Supply Chain Lab at the Berlin School of Economics and Law. His research spans supply chain resilience and digital supply chain twins. He has made influential contributions, particularly exploring structural dynamics of supply chains.

He delivered invited plenary, keynote, panel and guest talks at the conferences of INFORMS, IFPR, IFIP, IFAC, IEEE, DSI and POM, and over 40 universities worldwide. Ranked #1 worldwide in Supply Chain by ScholarGPS and #1 in Operations Research by Standford/Elsevier ranking.

Supply Chain Viability and Digital Ecosystems

This talk is devoted to supply chain viability and digital ecosystems. We will discuss adaptation-based principles of supply chain viability, design and implementation of digital technology for viable supply chains, and human-AI collaboration in digital twins and viability stress testing.

Teodor Gabriel Crainic

Université du Québec à Montréal & CIRRELT

Teodor Gabriel Crainic is Adjunct Professor of Operations Research, Transportation, and Logistics, School of Management, Université du Québec à Montréal, and Adjunct Professor of Operations Research, Department of Computer Science and Operations Research, Université de Montréal. He is also a senior scientist at CIRRELT, the Interuniversity Research Center for Enterprise Networks, Logistics, and Transportation.

The research interests of Professor Crainic are in network, integer, and combinatorial optimization, meta-heuristics, and parallel computing applied to the planning and management of complex systems. Major contributions targeted methods for national/regional planning, the design, scheduling, and operation management of consolidation-based carriers.
